Food Items Only

We focus exclusively on gathering physical, high-utility non-perishable food staples. No cash or financial donations are requested or accepted.

🏛️

Hindu Food Bank Partner

All collected provisions are handed over directly to the Hindu Food Bank in Mississauga for organized distribution.

Donation Guidelines

Please review what we can accept to ensure efficient sorting and safety for recipient families.

✓

Acceptable Non-Perishable Items

  • 🌾Rice, Flour & Pulses (Dals)
  • 🥣Cereal & Oatmeal
  • 🍝Pasta, Noodles & Pasta Sauces
  • 🥫Canned Fruits, Vegetables & Soups
  • 🫗Cooking Oil & Tomato Puree
  • 🍼Baby Milk Powder & Coffee/Tea Powder
  • 🍪Boxed Cookies / Biscuits
✕

What to Avoid

  • 💵No Money / Cash Donations (Strictly items only)
  • 🏺No Glass Containers
  • 🥬No Perishable or Fresh Goods
  • ⏳No Expired Food Items
  • 🍲No Home-Cooked or Home-Packed Items
